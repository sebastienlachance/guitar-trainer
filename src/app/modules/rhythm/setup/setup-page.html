Calibration

Sound takes time to leave the speakers and reach the microphone. Strike once on each of 8 clicks (after 4 to listen to) and the app measures that delay, so your timing is judged fairly. Calibrate again after changing speakers, headphones or device.

What to play

Any strike counts: strums, picked notes or muted chucks. Strike every subdivision, on the beat. In a pattern, D and U say which way to strum; the app hears when you strum, not which way, and a strike in a rest counts against the take.
